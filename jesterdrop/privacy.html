Privacy policy

JesterDrop

Effective October 15, 2026

JesterDrop is an iPhone arcade game. It does not collect personal data. It does not track you. It has no accounts and no third-party SDKs. Scores and settings stay on your iPhone.

Who makes this app

JesterDrop is made by KingAragorn, an individual developer on the Apple App Store.

Data the app does not collect

JesterDrop does not collect data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to KingAragorn or to anyone else.

  • The app does not track you across apps or websites.
  • The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sign you in.
  • The app does not include third-party SDKs, including kits for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • The app does not sell or share personal data. It has none to sell or share.

What stays on your iPhone

JesterDrop saves scores and settings only on the device, in the app’s local preferences (Apple’s UserDefaults). That information is not uploaded. If you delete the app, iOS deletes those scores and settings with it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions

Questions about this policy can go to the JesterDrop support page.
